How this map works

● Verified — merged into main, visible to everyone.

● Pending — submitted by someone, not yet reviewed by the owner. Only shown when you switch to "Verified + Pending".

● Adopt a Marker — donated waypoints with coordinates but no photo or write-up yet. Click one, fill in what you know, and it goes into the pending queue like a normal submission.

Click + Propose marker, then click a spot on the map. Fill in the form, attach a photo if you have one, and submit — no account or GitHub needed. It'll show up on the map once approved.

Owner mode

Connect your admin key

Everyone else's markers land in a pending queue automatically — no GitHub account, no pull requests. Paste your admin key here to publish your own markers instantly and to see Approve / Reject buttons on pending ones (switch to "Verified + Pending" to see them on the map).

The key is stored only in this browser's local storage and sent only to this site's own API.
